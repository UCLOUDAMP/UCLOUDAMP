## Home

UCloudAmp delivers founder-led systems engineering for governed AI execution, traceable automation, and operator control.

We design bounded systems that preserve explicit authority, scoped capabilities, validation, fail-closed behavior, provenance, versioned artifacts, and execution receipts.

<!-- §section: CONTENT | id: services -->
## Services

- Systems and hybrid-cloud architecture
- Bounded workflow automation
- AI pipeline design
- Operational evidence and AI-readiness assessments
- Operator documentation and handoff

<!-- §section: CONTENT | id: approach -->
## Approach

Every engagement is built around explicit authority boundaries, scoped execution capability, and validation-first gates.

Delivery emphasizes traceability from intent to result, with evidence and versioned artifacts that operators can inspect, hand off, and re-run.
